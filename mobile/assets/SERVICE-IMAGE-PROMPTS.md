# Picha za huduma — prompts

Picha nane, moja kwa kila huduma iliyo kwenye katalogi. Zinatumika kwenye
skrini ya "Chagua huduma", kadi za "Huduma zetu" kwenye Mwanzo, na kadi ya
"Huduma maalum".

## Jinsi ya kuzitumia

Kila prompt ina sehemu mbili: **STYLE** (ileile kila mara, usiibadilishe) na
**SUBJECT** (inabadilika). Kubandika STYLE bila kubadilisha ndiyo inayofanya
picha nane zifanane badala ya kuonekana zimetoka sehemu nane tofauti.

Omba **1:1 square, 1024×1024**. Hifadhi kwa majina yaliyo hapa chini ndani ya
`mobile/assets/services/`.

---

## STYLE (bandika mwanzoni mwa kila prompt)

```
Professional editorial healthcare photograph, East African home setting in
Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. Black African people only. Warm natural daylight
from a window, soft shadows, shallow depth of field. Nurse wears clean navy
blue medical scrubs with a stethoscope. Calm, dignified, respectful mood —
real care, not stock-photo smiling at camera. Tidy modest Tanzanian home
interior: plain walls, simple furniture, a little greenery. Photorealistic,
sharp focus on hands and faces, natural skin tones. No text, no logos, no
watermarks, no clinical hospital equipment. Square 1:1 composition.
```

## Baada ya kuzipata

Ziweke kwenye `mobile/assets/services/` kwa majina yaliyo juu. Zinakatwa na
kuwekwa kwenye skrini kiotomatiki — hakuna code itakayobadilika.

Kama picha moja itatoka tofauti na nyingine, iombe tena kwa STYLE ileile
badala ya kuirekebisha; kufanana ndiyo jambo la muhimu zaidi hapa, zaidi ya
ubora wa picha moja.
